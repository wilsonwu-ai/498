What just happened?

Your browser ran a line-for-line copy of the benchmark's own reference solutions, then compared the result to the benchmark's answer key with exact equality, the way the benchmark's tests do. Functions like sin, cos and tan are computed by a math library, and different libraries round the last of about 17 digits differently. When a test demands exact equality, one step in that last digit turns a pass into a fail.
